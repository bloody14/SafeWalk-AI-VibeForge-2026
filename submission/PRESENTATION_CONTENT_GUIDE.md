# SafeWalk AI — PowerPoint Content Guide for Teammate

Create a concise 6-slide presentation (3–4 minutes maximum). Use only actual screenshots and implemented features. Build the PPT during/after actual implementation; this guide is pre-contest content structure only.

## Slide 1 — Product

SafeWalk AI — AI-powered urban route awareness for people unfamiliar with a city.
One-line message: “Compare walking routes with more context than distance and travel time.”

## Slide 2 — The problem

A newcomer may not know local lighting conditions, locations of publicly reported incidents, pedestrian-activity patterns or the freshness of available observations. Conventional navigation is mainly time/distance-oriented.

## Slide 3 — The solution

Show the actual core journey: origin/destination → route options → condition indicators → AI explanation → journey completion → community feedback.

## Slide 4 — Product demonstration

Use actual screenshots: route search/map, route comparison, factor breakdown, completed journey/feedback and feedback shown in the app. Do not use old prototype screenshots as competition implementation evidence.

## Slide 5 — Technology and AI

Describe the actual chosen framework, map/routing provider, data labels, deterministic route-awareness calculation, server-side Gemini explanation if enabled, and feedback persistence. Clearly distinguish real integration from fallback/demo data.

## Slide 6 — Impact, boundaries and future

Explain how route-condition awareness and post-journey observations could help newcomers. State that the current prototype does not guarantee safety, predict crime or provide verified city-wide coverage. Future work: trusted city datasets, moderation and broader validation.
